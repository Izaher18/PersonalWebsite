01

Pulse

Pulse polls endpoints on a schedule, records the status code and response time of every check, and turns that history into uptime percentages you can publish.

The parts I wanted practice on are the ones class projects usually skip: a checker with timeouts and retries, Postgres queries that aggregate a month of checks instead of loading them into the app, and status pages served from a short cache so a burst of readers costs one set of queries.
